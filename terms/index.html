Free draft

I make you a free draft first. You don't pay anything until your site goes live. Your setup fee is due when it goes live, and monthly fees start then.

30-day money back

If you're not happy in the first 30 days after your site goes live, tell me and I'll refund your setup fee. That applies to either payment option. On the no-setup option, I refund the monthly fees you've paid.

Monthly plans

There's no early-leaving charge in the first 30 days.
